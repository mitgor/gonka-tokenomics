Column formulas (ALL must be Excel formulas referencing params/upstream -- never hardcode values):

**A (Period Label):** `={es_sheet}!{es_period_col}{es_row}` -- style "crossref_cell"

**B (CP Balance GNK):** Running waterfall balance.
- Period 0 (i==0): `={cp_ref}-{pol_alloc_ref}-{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365-C{row}`
  But wait -- C is CP Outflows, which we need for the waterfall display. Use a cleaner approach:
  - `={cp_ref}-{pol_alloc_ref}-C{row}` where C{row} is the period's total outflows excluding the initial POL draw (which is a one-time deduction)
  - Actually, the cleanest approach: B tracks the remaining balance after ALL draws. For period 0: `={cp_ref}-{pol_alloc_ref}-{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365`. For subsequent periods: `=B{row-1}-{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365`. This is the balance after POL (one-time at period 0) and defense (ongoing).
- Period 0: `={cp_ref}-{pol_alloc_ref}-{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365`
- Periods 1+: `=B{row-1}-{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365`
- Style: "tokens"

**C (CP Outflows GNK):** Shows period outflows for waterfall visibility.
- Period 0: `={pol_alloc_ref}+{defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365` (POL + defense draw)
- Periods 1+: `={defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365` (defense draw only)
- Style: "tokens"

**D (POL Revenue $):** Midpoint of low/high LP fee revenue, prorated, minus rebalancing cost.
`=({pol_rev_low_ref}+{pol_rev_high_ref})/2*{days}/365-{pol_rebal_ref}*{days}/365`
- Style: "currency"

**E (Cumulative POL Revenue $):** Running sum.
- Period 0: `=D{row}`
- Periods 1+: `=E{row-1}+D{row}`
- Style: "currency"

**F (Buyback Burn GNK):** Cross-ref from Token Price tab column K (already calculated there).
`={tp_sheet}!{tp_buyback_col}{tp_row}`
- Style: "tokens"

**G (Cumulative Burn GNK):** Running total.
- Period 0: `=F{row}`
- Periods 1+: `=G{row-1}+F{row}`
- Style: "tokens"

**H (Burn % of Supply):** `=G{row}/{total_supply_ref}`
- Style: "percent"

**I (AI Fund Inflows $):** Cross-ref from Fee Transition tab column L.
`={ft_sheet}!{ft_ai_fund_col}{ft_row}`
- Style: "currency"

**J (AI Fund Balance $):** Running balance: inflows minus expenses.
- Period 0: `=I{row}-{ai_fund_expenses_ref}*{days}/30` (expenses prorated from monthly to period)
  Note: For monthly periods (days=30), this is exactly 1x monthly expenses. For annual periods (days=365), this is ~12.17x monthly expenses.
- Periods 1+: `=J{row-1}+I{row}-{ai_fund_expenses_ref}*{days}/30`
- Style: "currency"

**K (Defense GNK Allocated):** Annual 6M GNK prorated.
`={defense_annual_ref}*{days}/365`
- Style: "tokens"

**L (Defense Treasury $):** GNK converted to USD at active price. Running sum (accumulation baseline -- no active spending in main timeline; scenario spending goes in below-data section).
- Period 0: `=K{row}*{tp_sheet}!{tp_active_col}{tp_row}`
- Periods 1+: `=L{row-1}+K{row}*{tp_sheet}!{tp_active_col}{tp_row}`
- Style: "currency"

**M (Net Treasury GNK):** Total GNK holdings = CP Balance + POL GNK allocation (still held as LP).
`=B{row}+{pol_alloc_ref}`
- Style: "tokens"

**N (Net Treasury $):** All assets in USD.
`=B{row}*{tp_sheet}!{tp_active_col}{tp_row}+{pol_alloc_ref}*{tp_sheet}!{tp_active_col}{tp_row}+E{row}+L{row}+J{row}`
This sums: CP balance in USD + POL GNK value in USD + cumulative POL fee revenue (USDC) + defense treasury (USDC) + AI Fund balance (USDC).
- Style: "currency"
